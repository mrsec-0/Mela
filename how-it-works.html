How Mela works

Find trusted help in Hawassa — for everyday home services and special events. Two different flows, one simple marketplace.

Home Services

Same-day help for everyday breakdowns.

  1. 1

    Browse or search by category

    Plumbing, electrical, cleaning and more — find what you need by neighbourhood or service type.

  2. 2

    Check who's available nearby

    See ratings, reviews and availability status at a glance. Filter by your neighbourhood.

  3. 3

    Call or WhatsApp directly

    No booking fee, no middleman. Reach the provider straight away and work out the details.

Events & Entertainment

Weddings, graduations, celebrations — booked by date.

  1. 1

    Browse by category

    Photographers, bands, MCs, decorators and caterers across Hawassa — city-wide, not neighbourhood-limited.

  2. 2

    Check portfolios and pricing

    View sample work, compare package options and see what each team offers before you reach out.

  3. 3

    Send a date inquiry

    Pick your event date, add a short message. The provider confirms or declines — you'll hear back by phone or WhatsApp.

For providers

Join Mela and let Hawassa find you.

  1. 1

    Verify your phone

    Quick SMS verification — no email or password needed.

  2. 2

    Set up your profile

    Add your bio, neighbourhood, rate or package pricing, and a photo. Events teams can upload a portfolio.

  3. 3

    Start appearing in search

    Once approved, customers can find your profile, contact you directly and leave reviews.

Free to join — no listing fees or commissions for now.
